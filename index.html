( 01 )Meet ultrarr

Model the account, not the pixels.

  1. ( 01 )

    Calls and screens are the richest record of the work. What matters is what the work did to the account: a champion goes quiet, usage drops, a renewal slips.

  2. ( 02 )

    ultrarr is built to model the people who keep and grow ARR by what their moves do to each account: the state before, the move, the state after.

( 02 )The challenge

Your best people work past the playbook. Agents only get the playbook.

  • ( Challenge 01 )

    A playbook is not the play.

    A playbook says how the work should go. Calls, screens and follow-ups show how your best people actually do it.

  • ( Challenge 02 )

    The best are proven by results.

    Who is best shows only when what people did is linked to what happened next, on accounts just as hard.

    • Expected
    • Result
    • Beat expected

  • ( Challenge 03 )

    Outcomes arrive late.

    Renewals land months after the calls that shaped them, so learning has to keep running until the renewal lands.

( 03 )Signals and moves

How ultrarr reads an account and the moves made on it.

Health, usage, tickets, stakeholders and contract dates come from the systems you already run. Moves are what a person did. ultrarr is built to label each one from calls and written work, then join what happened next.

One workspace for the team and the agents.

Your team and the agents share one workspace: what needs you comes first, then your accounts, where each agent posts a short note when something changes, with one move to offer.

An agent per account, working on its own.

Every account has its own agent. It replies in the account's chat and leaves notes on its own; what waits on a person comes first, and anything that reaches a customer goes through the approval gate.

( Depth )

Levels are earned per subject.

Each agent is built to earn a level per subject on evaluations, not per hour of training.

  1. I

    Foundations

    Passes the subject's knowledge evaluation. Drafts and suggests; a person sends everything.

  2. II

    Practitioner

    Beats the baseline on the subject's scenario evaluation. Acts alone on low-stakes tasks; a person approves the rest.

  3. III

    Expert

    Matches top-performer responses on the subject's evaluation. Leads in its subject and escalates outside it.
